Ember

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version.

  • Ember stores what you put into it (food, weight, training, photos) so that it can show it back to you. That is health information, and we only hold it because you ask us to.
  • It is stored in the European Union and only your account can read it.
  • When you use a feature that needs AI (describing or photographing a meal, reading a label, meal ideas, the assistant), what you send for that request goes to Anthropic, a company in the United States, to be answered.
  • No advertising and no tracking. We never sell your information, and the only companies that handle it are the ones listed below that help run Ember.
  • You can copy out everything, or delete your account and everything in it, from inside the app at any time.

Who is responsible

Ember is run by Peter Farrell, Castlerea, County Roscommon, Ireland, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact details will be added here before Ember opens beyond its invited test group.

What Ember holds

What Why
Your email address To sign you in. We email you only what signing in needs: a link to confirm your address, a sign-in link, or a link to reset your password.
Your password, if you set one To sign you in. It is kept only in a scrambled form that cannot be read back, by us or by anyone else.
Your profile: age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goals and targets To work out your calorie and nutrient targets.
Your diary: food and drink, water, caffeine, exercise, weigh-ins To show your day and your progress.
Your training: programmes, sessions, lifts, rehab plans, pain scores and recovery check-ins (sleep, soreness, energy, stress) To run your training and suggest when to push or rest.
Progress photos, if you add them To let you compare over time. They are private to you.
Foods and recipes you save, and reports that a product's figures are wrong To reuse them, and to correct the food data.
How much you have used the AI features each day (a count and a cost, not the content) To keep the service within its limits.
Error reports: the kind of error, where in the app's code it happened, the screen you were on and your browser type To fix faults. Quoted text and numbers are removed before a report is sent, so it cannot contain what you logged.

Much of this is information about your health, which the law treats as a special category. We hold it only with your explicit consent, which you give when you sign up and can withdraw at any time by deleting your account.

We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and Ember contains no analytics or tracking tools.

Squads: what other people can see

Nothing in your diary is visible to anyone else unless you join or start a squad. A squad is an invite-only group with one board for the week. If you join one, its members can see the name you choose for the board, your XP for the current week, your streak in days and how many training sessions you have done this week. They cannot see your food, your weight, your photos, your email address or anything else. You can leave a squad at any time, which removes you from its board at once, and deleting your account removes you from every squad. The person who started a squad can take a name off its board.

On your phone, reminders are set on the phone itself. We do not send them from a server and we do not keep a record of them.

Votes and suggestions

If you vote on what Ember should build next, we keep which items you voted for so that a vote counts once and you can take it back. Other people see only the totals, never who voted. If you send a suggestion, we keep its text with your account so the people who build Ember can read it; nobody else can. Both are deleted with your account.

A diary from another app

If you bring in a diary exported from another app, the file is read on your device. Only the days and entries that go into your diary are saved to your account; the file itself is not uploaded. You can take the imported days out again at any time.

A recipe from a link

If you bring a recipe in from a web page, the address you paste is sent to our server, which fetches the page and reads the recipe's name, servings and ingredients from it. The site sees a request from Ember, not from you. We do not keep a list of the pages you ask for; the recipe you save keeps a link to its page.

Why we are allowed to use it

  • To provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract): your email address, profile and everything you log.
  • Your explicit consent, for the health information in what you log, and for sending AI requests to Anthropic.
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping the app working: error reports and usage counts.

Anthropic is outside the European Economic Area. Requests are sent under its commercial terms, which include the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, and it does not use them to train its models. If you would rather nothing went to Anthropic, do not use the AI features: searching, scanning barcodes and logging by hand do not use it.

On your device

Ember does not use cookies. It keeps a few things in your browser's storage on your device: your sign-in, a copy of your recent diary so the app opens without a connection, and entries waiting to be sent. Signing out removes the copy of your diary from that device.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your diary, photos and sign-in are deleted straight away and cannot be recovered. Error reports are deleted with the account. Anthropic keeps AI requests for a limited period under its own terms.

Your rights

Under data protection law you can:

  • See and copy your information: in the app under You, Your data, Copy my data as JSON.
  • Correct it: by editing it in the app.
  • Delete it: in the app under You, Your data, Delete my account.
  • Withdraw your consent, object to or restrict our use of it, by contacting us or deleting your account.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie), or to the regulator where you live.

Age

Ember is for adults. You must be 18 or over to use it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
