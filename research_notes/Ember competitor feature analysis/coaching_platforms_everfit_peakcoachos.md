# Coaching platforms: Everfit and "Peach Peak Coach OS" (identity uncertain)

Research date: 24 November 2026. All facts are as read on that date unless a different date is given. Prices are in US dollars as published. "Via search summary" means the page itself was not opened (usually because it returned HTTP 403) and the fact comes from a search-engine summary of it: treat those as lower confidence.

## 1. Which product is "Peach Peak Coach OS"?

### Takeaway
No product with the literal name "Peak Coach OS", "PeakCoachOS" or "Peach Coach" was found. The two realistic matches are **peak/performance** (peakperformance.to, Dubai, tagline "The Integrated Coaching Operating System") and **CoachOS** (os-coach.com). Confidence is low to moderate: roughly 50% peak/performance, 30% CoachOS, 20% something else or two separate products named in one breath. The owner should confirm which was meant; both are covered below, peak/performance in most depth.

### Cited Findings
- Exact-name searches for "Peak Coach OS", "PeakCoachOS", "PeakCoach" and "Peak Coach" returned no product of that name; results were only "Coach OS"/"CoachOS" listings and unrelated "Peak" apps — [Capterra IE listing "coach os" (appeared in results, page not opened)](https://www.capterra.ie/software/1109880/coach-os); [GetApp listing "coach os" (returned 403 when opened)](https://www.getapp.ie/software/2141898/coach-os)

- **CoachOS** (os-coach.com) is "fitness coaching software for online coaches", tagline "More time coaching. Less time chasing.", with an AI Copilot, a free plan for up to 10 clients and paid plans at $30 and $60 a month — [CoachOS home](https://os-coach.com/)
- A product listed as "coach os" appears on Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice, described as client management software for independent personal trainers and fitness coaches managing 5 to 100+ clients, with client tracking, program builder, automated follow-ups and client mobile apps, on cloud, iPhone, Android and iPad (via search summary; the listing pages returned 403) — [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/coach-os/); [Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1109880/coach-os)

### Inferences
- "Peach Peak" reads like one word heard twice by the dictation engine ("Peak", first mis-heard as "Peach"), followed by "Coach OS". That favours a single product whose name or tagline contains both "Peak" and "coaching operating system": peak/performance is the only one found that fits both halves.
- Against that reading: peak/performance is very new (UAE launch stated as July 2026, still showing a waitlist) and has no independent reviews, so the owner is most likely to have met it through its own Instagram, TikTok or YouTube marketing. CoachOS matches the "Coach OS" half exactly but has no "Peak" in its name.
- A third reading is that the owner named two or three separate apps ("Peach", "Peak", "CoachOS"). If so, CoachOS (os-coach.com) is the coach-platform one; the "Peach" and "Peak" names found are consumer or team-sport apps.
- It is not established whether the "coach os" on Capterra/GetApp is the same company as os-coach.com. The descriptions are consistent (program builder, automated follow-ups, client apps), but the listing pages could not be opened to check the vendor website.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm the intended product. Needs one question to the owner: "Did you mean peak/performance from Dubai (peakperformance.to), or CoachOS (os-coach.com), or something else?"
- Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice pages for "coach os" all returned 403, so vendor URL, founding year, ratings and review text for that listing are unverified.

## 2. Company, country, founding, funding, team and platforms

Everfit is an established, venture-backed US company (San Francisco HQ, launched August 2020, about 100 staff in 2024) with coach web and mobile apps, a client app on iOS, Android and Apple Watch, and a white-label option on Enterprise. peak/performance is a founder-led Dubai start-up at launch stage with no published funding, customer count or verified store listing. CoachOS is a small product with one named founder and no published company details.

## 7. What is distinctive about each?

### Takeaway
Everfit's distinguishing mix is a free 5-client tier, a strong workout builder, Autoflow for running many clients on one schedule, and a fast-growing AI suite, sold à la carte. peak/performance's pitch is one branded, AI-led "operating system" that also replaces Zoom, the CRM and the marketing stack, with a clinical angle (hormone testing, biomarkers), but it is unproven. CoachOS's angle is an AI copilot that tells the coach who needs attention, at a low flat price.

### Cited Findings
- Everfit positions itself on "all-in-one", "AI and automation", breadth of niches, and global multilingual reach — [Everfit llms.txt](https://everfit.io/llms.txt)
- Everfit-specific items not seen on the other two: Autoflow by exact date or day sequence with usage-based pricing; AI push-up challenge with live rep counting; automated challenge leaderboards; Marketplace with AI matchmaking (beta); HSA/FSA payments; Olly AI Assistant and Olly Voice; a free Academy with CECs — [Everfit automation](https://everfit.io/automation/); [Everfit AI Suite](https://everfit.io/ai-suite/); [Everfit features](https://everfit.io/features/); [Everfit llms.txt](https://everfit.io/llms.txt)
- peak/performance says it "replaces six" tools and a "$400–700 / mo" stack, and claims to be "the only coaching platform in the GCC" with AI built into its core; it claims check-ins "6× faster" — [peak/performance home](https://peakperformance.to/)
- peak/performance-specific items: native WebRTC 1:1 and group video, annotated video form-checks and AI form-check video, Pricing AI, churn intervention prompts, marketing studio with AI templates, course builder with gating, multi-tenant white label with custom domains, a Dutch hormone-testing module, corporate wellness module — [peak/performance platform](https://peakperformance.to/platform); [peak/performance compare](https://peakperformance.to/compare); [peak/performance pricing](https://peakperformance.to/pricing)
- CoachOS-specific items: "21 AI pattern detectors", a "focus queue" of clients needing attention, a "what changed since last week" summary per client, a supplement library with per-client protocols, and "no per-module add-ons" — [CoachOS features](https://os-coach.com/features); [CoachOS home](https://os-coach.com/)

### Inferences
- The three sit at different points: Everfit is the broad incumbent with modular pricing; peak/performance is a premium, all-inclusive challenger aimed at the Gulf and at coaches with at least 10 clients; CoachOS is a lightweight AI-first tool for small rosters.
- The common AI pattern across all three is the same: draft or summarise for the coach, with the coach approving before anything reaches the client (Everfit Smart Response and meal plan generator, peak/performance "coach final approval", CoachOS pre-reply summary).
- Everfit's most-cited weakness (nutrition) is the area it invested in most during 2026 (MacroSnap, micronutrients, AI meal plans), so reviews older than mid-2026 understate its current nutrition tooling.
- For Irish and UK users specifically, none of the three publishes a named food database or states UK/Irish product coverage, so the quality of barcode and food matching for local products is unknown.

### Gaps
- No independent evidence exists for peak/performance's efficiency and "only platform in the GCC" claims.
- How well MacroSnap performs on UK and Irish packaged foods, and which database backs barcode lookups: not published.
- Whether the owner meant peak/performance, CoachOS or another product remains the main open question for this whole note.
