# Coaching platforms: HubFit and Kahunas (state as of 24 November 2026)

Research date: 24 November 2026. Vendor pages were read on that date and describe the current product. Anything older is dated inline. Both vendors' own sites are marketing sources: feature claims below are what the vendor says, not independently tested. Capterra, G2, Reddit, YouTube and Google Play could not be read directly (see Gaps in each section).

## 1. Identification and company facts (who they are, where, how big, which platforms)

### Takeaway
"Kahuna's" is Kahunas (kahunas.io), a fitness coaching platform founded by Mark Fox and run from the UAE; the identification is confirmed. HubFit now lives at hubfit.com (hubfit.io redirects there) and is also a UAE-registered company; neither publishes funding, and both went through major relaunches in autumn 2026 (HubFit App 3.0 in September, Kahunas relaunch with per-seat pricing and a web-app client on 28 October).

### Inferences
- Both companies are small, founder-led, UAE free-zone entities selling mainly to English-speaking coaches (Kahunas has a visible UK lineage: PureGym endorsement, UK-heavy Trustpilot and App Store white-label apps for UK coaches). Neither is an Irish or UK company on paper today.
- Kahunas's 15-person team page with five "Coach Performance Partners" (onboarding and support staff) suggests a high-touch support model; the founder holds every technical and product title, which implies a very small engineering team.
- HubFit's "50,000+ coaches" almost certainly counts free accounts (it has a free plan); Kahunas's "15,000+ coaches" comes from a product with no free plan. The two numbers are not comparable.

### Gaps
- No funding information was found for either company. No headcount for HubFit. No HubFit founder is named on the pages read; the only name found is the App Store provider (Abdulrahman Ismail), which may or may not be the founder.
- Kahunas founding year: one directory says "around 2021"; the App Store ID and a UK lockdown origin story are consistent with 2020 to 2022, but no primary source gives the year. No Companies House record was checked, so any earlier UK incorporation is unverified.
- HubFit founding year (2023) comes from directories only.
- Data hosting region is not stated in Kahunas's terms; HubFit's was not checked.

## 2. What the coach gets (feature inventory)

### Takeaway
Both cover the standard coaching stack (programs, nutrition, check-ins, habits, messaging, payments, automations, communities, challenges, teams). HubFit's coach-side differentiators are native apps, a 5,000+ video exercise library, 5,000+ recipes, a generous challenge engine, and openness (MCP server for Claude/ChatGPT and a REST API). Kahunas's are depth of check-in review, its "Crew" AI assistants and attention scoring, lab-report and menstrual-cycle tracking, pose-tracking exercise analysis, courses, and PayPal alongside Stripe.

### Inferences
- Neither platform has built-in video calling or a public booking page; both expect coaches to keep Zoom/Calendly-type tools. Neither has a real sales CRM; Kahunas lists "Leads" but it was not examined.

- Both keep AI away from the client: in both products AI drafts or summarises for the coach and nothing reaches a client without the coach accepting it. Neither offers a client-facing AI assistant or AI food logging on the pages read.
- HubFit's "custom branding" appears to be branding inside the shared HubFit app rather than a separately published store app; Kahunas's "white label" is now a branded web app. A true coach-named App Store listing is not clearly offered to new customers by either in late 2026.

### Gaps
- HubFit: no page states whether a separately published white-label store app can be bought; whether questionnaires are distinct from check-in forms; file or video storage limits; which plan Workout Studio content can be sold on (the page describes no paywall for on-demand content).
- Kahunas: the Leads, Group chats, Communities, Education, Challenges, Team hub, Custom domain, Recipes, Supplement plans, Screen recorder and Personalisation pages were not read in full; details above for those come from the home and pricing pages only.
- Neither vendor names the food database provider or the AI model used.

## 3. What the client gets in the app

### Takeaway
HubFit clients get native iOS and Android apps (rebuilt as "App 3.0" in September 2026) with guided logging, automatic PRs, a built-in food tracker with barcode scanning, and wide wearable coverage. Kahunas clients now get a link-installed web app with offline logging, rest timers, a 1.5-million-food tracker with barcode scanning, and wearables partly routed through a separate companion app; neither offers photo or AI food logging on the pages read.

- Kahunas: photo comparison beyond check-in-to-check-in comparison, superset behaviour in the client logger, and whether barcode scanning works well in the web app are not documented on the pages read.

## 4. Pricing

### Takeaway

## 6. What is distinctive about each (why a coach would switch)

### Takeaway
A coach switches to HubFit for a free start, lower flat pricing at 25 to 500 clients, polished native apps, challenges and broad wearables, and open AI access through MCP and an API. A coach switches to Kahunas for built-in AI triage and assistants, deep check-in review, labs, cycle tracking, pose-tracking form analysis, courses, PayPal and offline payments, and very low per-seat cost at large scale.

### Cited Findings
- HubFit: free plan for up to 2 clients with no card, client app always free for clients — [HubFit pricing](https://hubfit.com/pricing)
- HubFit: MCP server on every paid plan working with six assistants, with coach approval before changes; REST API and webhooks on Ultimate — [HubFit Scale](https://hubfit.com/platform/scale); [HubFit changelog](https://hubfit.com/changelog)

Both are coach-first tools where the client app exists to serve the coach's workflow; neither is usable by a member without a coach.
- Both are priced in US dollars and run from the UAE; neither markets EU data residency.
- The main strategic contrast is HubFit's "flat tiers, open platform, native apps" against Kahunas's "per seat, AI inside, web app". Kahunas's model makes sophisticated features contingent on roster size, which is the opposite of HubFit's small-coach-friendly ladder.

### Gaps
- No independent head-to-head review (Reddit, YouTube or trade press) was found; the only direct comparison is HubFit's own marketing page.
- Client retention, churn or engagement figures for either platform are vendor marketing claims without methodology (for example HubFit's "3x longer" and "40% more engagement") and were not treated as findings.
