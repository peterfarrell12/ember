Research date: 24 November 2026. App Store pages show dates without a year for the current year; where a date below has no year it is read as 2026 and flagged. ## 1. What exactly are these two products (identification, developer, country, launch date, website, store listings)?

### Takeaway
"Parrot Cal" is almost certainly **ParrotPal: Calorie Tracker / Calorie Counter** (ParrotPal Ltd, London, UK; founder Scott Flear; iPhone only), and "Oxcal.app" is almost certainly **0xCal: AI Calorie Counter** (zero-x-Cal, website 0xcal.app; solo developer Bartłomiej "Bartek" Pichalski, Poland; iPhone only). No product literally called "ParrotCal", "Parrot Cal" or "OxCal" (as a calorie app) was found.

### Inferences
- Confidence that "Parrot Cal" means ParrotPal: high (roughly 90%). It is a UK-built calorie counter with strong UK reviews, the kind of product an Irish owner would meet, and "Pal" to "Cal" is an easy dictation slip. No rival candidate exists.
- Confidence that "Oxcal.app" means 0xCal (0xcal.app): high (roughly 85 to 90%). The owner dictated a domain name, and 0xcal.app is the only calorie product at that domain shape; a speech recogniser would write "zero x cal" or "oh x cal" as "Oxcal".
- The two products are very different in scale and style: ParrotPal is a funded UK company with coaching content and a long quiz; 0xCal is a solo-built, design-first, iOS-native utility. The owner being "really impressed" with both probably reflects two different strengths (coaching and habit framing versus speed and native polish).
- Alternatives considered and set aside: Cal AI (the category leader, "Cal" in the name but no parrot or ox); OpenCal, xCal, Calyx, Caloryx (similar-sounding small apps that appeared in search but none uses the oxcal.app domain); OxCal the radiocarbon calibration software from Oxford (not a calorie app).

### Gaps
- oxcal.app (letter O) returned HTTP 503 on two attempts, so it could not be confirmed whether that exact domain is unregistered, a redirect to 0xcal.app, or an unrelated site. The identification rests on the 0xcal.app match.
- No exact first App Store release date was found for 0xCal. Its app id and a v1.4.3 fix in March 2026 suggest a release in the second half of 2025, but this is not verified.
- ParrotPal's funding amount is not published on its site; no press coverage of a round was retrieved.
- No founder interviews, Reddit threads or YouTube walkthroughs were retrieved for either app within the search budget.

## 2. What is every feature each offers?

### Takeaway
ParrotPal is a chat-first logger (voice, text, photo, barcode, label) wrapped in a coaching programme: Coach PAL daily messages, weekly check-ins that reset targets, a LeanShield score, challenges, podcasts, workouts, GLP-1 support and 16,000+ recipes. 0xCal is a lean iOS-native logger (chat with "Gram", photo, voice, barcode, LiDAR portion measuring) with fasting, water and caffeine, Apple Watch, widgets, Siri, meal planning and data import, and deliberately no coaching content, account or social layer.

## 5. What is distinctive about the design and onboarding?

### Takeaway
ParrotPal uses the standard high-converting pattern (23-step personalising quiz, rating prompt, hard paywall with trial, then a 7-day challenge) but wraps it in a chat-first home screen and a humane "plan bends with real life" tone. 0xCal's distinctiveness is native craft: dark-mode-first minimal interface, one logging sheet for everything, transparent gram assumptions, red and green budget cards, no account, and deep use of iOS hardware and system features.

### Cited Findings

**ParrotPal**
- Onboarding recorded at 23 steps: a long personalising quiz from about 0:14, a rating warm-up prompt at about 1:34, a hard paywall at about 2:28, the chat home screen at about 2:56, and a 7-Day Challenge with daily tasks and podcasts at about 4:22 (teardown undated) — [screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/ai-calorie-counter-parrotpal)
- The single input field combining text, voice, photo and barcode is called out as the core design decision; complex actions are moved to a "+" menu to keep the main screen clean — [screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/ai-calorie-counter-parrotpal)
- Positioning line: "Lose weight and stay strong. Just tell ParrotPal what you ate, and your plan bends when life gets busy"; "Not a diet" — [parrotpal.com](https://parrotpal.com)
- Coach tone is user-selectable: Gentle or Tough Love — [parrotpal.com](https://parrotpal.com)
- Honesty framing on the site: photos labelled as adjustable estimates, sample screens labelled "illustrative", LeanShield "Not a medical test", and a planned randomised trial described as "in design. It hasn't run yet, so we don't claim a result" — [parrotpal.com](https://parrotpal.com)
- Home page was redesigned around voice, chat, photo and barcode logging in v4.2.7 to 4.2.8 (May, read as 2026), charts redesigned in v4.2.9 (1 June), and the 2.0 release (November 2026) promises more customisation — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6462694790); [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6462694790)

**0xCal**
- "Dark mode first. Minimal UI"; tagline "The calorie tracker that finally belongs on your phone" — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/0xcal)
- Marketed on being lightweight (description says 25 MB against much larger rivals; current size 56.5 MB) — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6749210009)
- Onboarding sets targets from Apple Health data (height, weight, age) plus activity level and goals, reducing manual entry; commenters praised the onboarding — [Product Hunt, comments page 2](https://www.producthunt.com/products/0xcal?page=2)

- System integrations as design features: widgets, Apple Watch, Siri and Shortcuts, Screen Time fasting shield, LiDAR Ruler+ — [App Store Ireland](https://apps.apple.com/ie/app/id6749210009); [0xcal.app](https://www.0xcal.app)

- Maker's stated philosophy: accuracy "enough" to tell whether you are on track, since manual logging is the main reason people quit — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/0xcal); [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6749210009)

### Inferences
- What likely impressed an owner building a UK and Ireland calorie and coaching app: from ParrotPal, the one-box chat logging, the forgiving modes (Pause, Holiday, Restart) that stop a missed week from wrecking averages or streaks, the short daily coach message with a chosen tone, the weekly check-in that resets targets, and the single LeanShield score; from 0xCal, the speed and restraint of the interface, transparent estimates, a streak that tolerates late logging, and no sign-up.
- Both apps label AI output as estimates and invite correction, which is close to the "Estimate" labelling approach; neither was found presenting AI figures as verified.
- ParrotPal leans on a hard paywall after a long quiz; 0xCal leans on low price and no account. These are opposite acquisition models, and reviews show the cost of the first one (trial and billing complaints).

- 0xCal's onboarding length, quiz content and paywall placement are undocumented in any source found; a hands-on install is needed.
